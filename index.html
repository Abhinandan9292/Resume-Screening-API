†

A note on match scores

A score is a search aid, not a judgement.

A match score compares the skills a recruiter searches for with the skills listed on a student’s profile. It doesn’t evaluate experience, projects or anything else in the résumé, and every result shows which of the searched skills were found, so a shortlist is always easy to explain.
